Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This policy explains how Skorax LLC ("Skorax", "we", "us") handles information when you visit www.skorax.com or play our mobile games.

Who we are

Skorax LLC is a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, United States. Our registered address is 30 N Gould St #47113, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. You can reach us at support@skorax.com.

Information we collect

When you email us. We receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

On this website. We don't use cookies, analytics or forms. Like any website, the services that host and deliver its files, such as our web host, GitHub and Google, receive your IP address and basic browser information when the page loads.

In our games. Our games don't require an account, and we don't ask for your name, email address or phone number in them. To show ads, our games use advertising services from partners such as Google AdMob and Gadsme. These services may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and the approximate location based on it
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version
  • app activity, such as the ads you see and tap and how you use the game

How we use information

  • to answer your messages and provide support
  • to show ads in our games, which fund their development
  • to understand how our games perform and fix problems
  • to prevent fraud and meet legal obligations

Advertising partners

Our advertising partners handle the data they collect under their own privacy policies and may use it to show you personalized ads. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings, usually under Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. Depending on where you live, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Sharing

We share information only with the advertising and service providers described above, when the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of our business.

How long we keep information

We keep emails for as long as we need them to handle your request and keep business records. Our advertising partners keep data according to their own policies.

Children

Our games are rated for teens and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. To make a request, email support@skorax.com. For data held by an advertising partner, use your device's ad settings or contact that partner.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no method of storing or sending data is completely secure.

International transfers

Skorax LLC is registered in the United States. We and our service providers may process information in other countries.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
